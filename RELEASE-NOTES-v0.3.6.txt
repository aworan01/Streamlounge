v0.3.6 — Native-feed entry point and ClearKey diagnostics

- Watched button: authorized M3U URL, native tvOS playback engines only; no browser or Cloudflare bypass; no implied Watched API/login support.
- Import reports if a web/login/Cloudflare page was returned in place of an M3U feed.
- ClearKey reports exact byte length for malformed keys without exposing secrets; playback stops with a clear status when a channel has no valid key.
- Unsupported HLS ClearKey labeling no longer results in misleading playback.
- Persist asynchronous ClearKey license errors instead of overwriting them with generic buffering status.
- No confirmed real-stream ClearKey playback yet.
